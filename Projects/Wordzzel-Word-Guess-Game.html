wordZzle Word Guess Game

E-Stone Technology Internship

Developed an Andoid App WordZzle and deployed on Playstore as part of E-Stone Technology Internship

If you are a fan of word puzzle games, WordZzle is just the perfect game for you. This game can be played by all ages from kids to adults both to test and improve your vocabulary skills as well as a source of entertainment and relaxation.

Have you ever finished your daily Wordle, the popular word guessing game and wished for more ! Welcome to WordZzle – Here you can play WordZzle unlimited times.

WordZzle is a free and fun Word Puzzle Game with Unlimited words and options for 4,5 and 6 letters that can be played by all ages. 

Challenge your mind and test your spelling and vocabulary skills for a truly addictive experience.

Game Rule

The goal of the game is to Guess the Word in 6 tries. You can start by randomly guessing any word of your choice. After each guess, the game will give a clue by changing the colour of the tiles to reflect how close you are to guess the correct word. It will tell you if each letter is in the word and if it is in its correct position.

A correct letter in the correct position is indicated with a green background. A correct letter in the wrong position is indicated with a yellow background and if the letter is not in the word at all it is indicated with a red background. At the endof 6 tries, if not already guessed, it will show the correct word.

You can click on Replay Option to play again.
